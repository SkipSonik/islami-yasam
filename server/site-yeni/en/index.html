FAQ

If you have another question, write to us.

Is Namaz Yolu free?

Yes. The free version shows ads; with a one-time payment you can remove ads and unlock all image messages.

Are prayer times the same as Diyanet?

In Türkiye they are calculated with the method used by the Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet). Elsewhere your country's common method is chosen automatically; you can change it in settings.

Does it work offline?

Prayer times, qibla and notifications work offline. Quran pages and surahs are downloaded on first open and saved on the phone.

Do I have to share my location?

No. Without location permission you can pick your city from the list and still get correct times.

Where is my data kept?

Prayer tracking, fasting, khatm and settings stay only on your phone. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Why are notifications delayed?

Some phones restrict apps to save battery. Use the notification reliability card in the app to enable the needed permissions in one tap.
