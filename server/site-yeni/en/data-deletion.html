Data Deletion

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Namaz Yolu needs no account, so there is no account to delete; here is how to delete your data.

1. Data on your phone

Prayer tracking, khatm, fasting, settings, location choice, dhikr counts and similar data stay only on your phone. To delete: Settings → Apps → Namaz Yolu → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.

2. Data on our server

Only if you used group khatm is there a record on our server. To have it deleted, email akingocler@gmail.com with:

  • Subject: “Namaz Yolu data deletion”
  • The khatm code (6 characters) and what to delete: the whole khatm or only your name
  • Brief proof that you created it or took a juz (e.g. khatm title, intention text, your juz)

We answer within 30 days, usually much sooner.

3. Advertising data

You can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Google → Ads on your phone, and change in-app ad consent under Settings → About → Ad consent preferences.

4. Website

Change your cookie choice via “Cookie preferences” in the footer, or clear your browser data to remove the record.

5. Other requests

For access, correction or objection requests under GDPR/KVKK, write to akingocler@gmail.com. See the Privacy Policy.
