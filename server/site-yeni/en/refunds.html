Purchases & Refunds

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Pre-contract information for the Premium (ad-free) purchase.

1. Seller

Akın GÖÇLER (“Göçler Store”). Contact: akingocler@gmail.com. Sales are made through Google Play; Google collects the payment.

2. Product

Namaz Yolu Premium is a digital product bought with a one-time payment: it removes ads and unlocks additional content. Price and taxes are shown on the purchase screen before you pay.

3. Payment and delivery

You pay with the payment method saved in Google Play; we never receive your card details. The content is activated immediately after purchase.

4. Right of withdrawal

For digital content supplied immediately, the statutory right of withdrawal may not apply once performance has begun with your consent (for example under Turkish distance-selling rules and EU Directive 2011/83/EU). Your confirmation of the purchase counts as that consent. Google Play's refund policy and any mandatory rights you have as a consumer remain unaffected.

5. How to request a refund

Refund requests are made through Google Play (Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history → the purchase → request a refund, or Google Play Help). If you have a problem, write to us with your order number and we will consider every reasonable request.

6. Restoring a purchase

On another device with the same Google account, use “Restore purchase” in the app to re-activate Premium.

7. Complaints

You may send complaints to us and may also contact your local consumer authority or court.
