Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

In short: Namaz Yolu needs no account. Your prayer tracking, khatm, fasting and settings stay on your phone. Your location is used on the device for prayer times and the qibla. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob.

1. Controller and contact

The app and site are published on Google Play under the developer account “Göçler Store”. Data controller: Akın GÖÇLER (individual developer). Contact: akingocler@gmail.com. This page also serves as our notice under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Law No. 6698) and the EU/UK GDPR; the Turkish-language KVKK notice is the Turkish original.

2. Data that stays on your device

Settings and theme, chosen location, prayer and qada tracking, fasting calendar, personal khatm, reading plan, dhikr counts, badges and zakat amounts are stored only on your phone and are not sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

3. Location

With your permission, location is used on the device to calculate prayer times, find the qibla and show the city name. If you use Nearby mosques, your location is rounded to about 100 m and sent to our server (namazyolu.com) and to Overpass (OpenStreetMap) servers. Our server does not link it to you and caches mosque lists for ~11 km areas for 30 days. Without location permission the app works with a city you choose.

4. Group khatm

If you create or join a group khatm, our server stores its title and intention, the optional name you enter, the juz you take and a random app-generated device ID. People with the same code can see names and juz; the device ID is never shown. To limit abuse we keep a shortened, irreversible hash of your IP address. To delete a khatm or name, see Data Deletion.

5. Gold price

If you tap “Fetch current price” in the zakat calculator, our server receives only your currency (e.g. TRY) and returns the gram-gold price.

6. Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect advertising ID, device information, approximate location (from IP), ad interactions and diagnostics to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland see Google's consent form; you can change your choice under Settings → About → Ad consent preferences. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience. See how Google uses data from partner sites and apps. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone settings.

7. Website namazyolu.com

The site sets no tracking or advertising cookies without your consent. If you accept, Google AdSense uses third-party cookies to serve ads (see the Cookie Policy). Fonts are served from our own server; no external font service is used. Our hosting provider may keep technical server logs (IP address, browser information, request time) for security.

8. Purchases

The ad-free Premium purchase is made entirely through Google Play. We never see or store your payment details; the app only learns from Google Play whether a purchase exists. See Purchases & Refunds.

9. Notifications

Prayer, suhoor/iftar, hadith-of-the-day and reading reminders are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to a server for this.

10. Quran text, audio and Kaaba stream

Quran text, translations and recitation audio are downloaded from alquran.cloud / islamic.network, which can see your IP address during these requests. The live Kaaba stream plays via YouTube and is subject to YouTube's privacy policy.

11. Legal bases (GDPR) and sharing

Consent (location permission, ad consent, cookies); performance of a contract (group khatm, Premium); legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention). We do not sell personal data. Recipients are limited to Google, our hosting provider, OpenStreetMap Overpass, alquran.cloud/islamic.network and YouTube. Some are located outside your country (including the USA); transfers rely on your consent for ads and on the safeguards provided by those providers. All connections use HTTPS.

12. Retention

On-device data stays until you delete it. Group khatm records are kept while the khatm is active or until you ask for deletion; long-unused khatms may be deleted. The mosque cache is deleted after 30 days.

13. Children

The app is for a general audience. The children's sections in the ablution and prayer guide are meant for parents teaching their children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13; tell us and we will delete it.

14. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, and object to processing, by writing to akingocler@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. You may also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (kvkk.gov.tr) or your local supervisory authority.

15. Changes

When this policy changes, the new date appears on this page.
