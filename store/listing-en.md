## Full description
Islami Yasam brings everything you need for daily worship into one clean app: accurate prayer times, the Holy Quran, qibla, Ramadan tools and much more.

🕌 PRAYER TIMES & ADHAN ALERTS
• Precise prayer times for your location, calculated offline
• The right calculation method for your country is chosen automatically
• Reliable prayer notifications even when the app is closed, plus early reminders
• Choose your alert sound: bell, chime, "Allah", Bismillah or Takbir
• Countdown to the next prayer and a 30-day timetable

📖 HOLY QURAN
• Read in the Madinah mushaf page layout
• Follow along while listening: the recited verse is highlighted on the page
• Verse-by-verse recitation by renowned reciters, controllable from the lock screen
• Translations in 9 languages
• Quran reading plan: finish in 30, 60 or 90 days with daily reminders

🤝 GROUP KHATM
• Track your personal khatm juz by juz
• Create a group khatm, share the code with family and friends and let everyone take a juz
• See who is reading which juz in real time

✅ PRAYER TRACKER & BADGES
• Mark each prayer with a single tap
• Keep your streak of days with all five prayers
• Weekly summary, missed-prayer (qada) tracker and badges

🌙 RAMADAN
• Ramadan timetable with suhoor and iftar countdown
• Suhoor reminder and iftar notification
• Fasting calendar and missed-fast tracking

🧭 QIBLA & MOSQUES
• Tilt-compensated qibla compass
• Nearby mosques with directions

📚 GUIDES & DUAS
• Illustrated wudu and prayer guide for men, women, boys and girls
• Prayer duas with Arabic, transliteration and meaning
• Morning-evening, meal, travel, healing and family duas with sources
• After-prayer tasbihat counter and dhikr counter
• 99 Names of Allah with meanings
• Hadith of the day

📅 AND MORE
• Islamic holidays and holy nights with countdown
• Zakat calculator with live gold price
• Live broadcast from Makkah and Madinah
• 5 themes: Emerald, Night, Iznik, Madinah and Kaaba
• Turkish, English, Arabic, German, French, Russian, Indonesian, Urdu and Japanese
